# Baarish ki pehli shaam — storylines (60 s, 1920x1080, music only)

Song map: intro 0–13 s (instrumental) · verse 13–37 s (vocals 12.9–19.4, 28.6–) · chorus 37–56 s (peak 45.3 s) · outro 56–60 s.

## 1. Blocky Monsoon (recommended: the owner's own character is the hero, and a voxel world is crisp to build)
Device: a character does the work. Form: a flight through one world.
The owner's 3D Minecraft character runs through a blocky rainy village to a glowing tea stall, meets a girl
(new blocky character), they share two cups of chai, the rain turns golden in the chorus, and the film ends
with both under one umbrella.
First 2 s: a single voxel raindrop falls toward the camera and splashes into a chai cup.
Beats: rain over blocky rooftops → he runs in (13 s, first line) → she arrives, soaked, smiling → two cups,
steam rises → chorus: lanterns light up street by street, camera cranes up (peak 45 s) → outro: one umbrella, title.
Look: deep indigo rain night, warm amber lamp light, voxel blocks, rain streaks, puddle reflections.
Sound: the song itself; a soft rain bed under the intro.

## 2. Baarish Diary
Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take down a painted street.
A watercolour-and-ink city in the rain; the camera never cuts, gliding from rooftops to a tea stall, two
umbrellas, two cups, a window where rain draws a heart.
First 2 s: a raindrop lands in a chai cup; its ripple becomes the title.
Beats: rooftops → tea stall → umbrellas meet → chai → chorus: city lights bloom → window, "hamesha tumhara".
Look: indigo/teal paper, ink outlines, amber light, watercolour bleed.

## 3. Raindrop Lyrics
Device: type carries the song. Form: match cuts.
The lyrics fall as raindrops in Devanagari and Roman, splash into puddles; circles match-cut: raindrop →
cup rim → street lamp → moon → ring.
First 2 s: the first word drops from the top and splashes.
Look: black-blue night, one warm accent, big Devanagari type.
