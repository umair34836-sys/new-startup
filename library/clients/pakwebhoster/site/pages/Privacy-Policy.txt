Privacy Policy for Quick CPanel Login Chrome Extension

The Quick CPanel Login Chrome extension allows users to save and manage their cPanel account credentials locally on their device for faster login.

Data Collected

The extension does not transmit any personal information to our servers. All stored data—including account name, URL, username, and password—is encrypted locally using AES-GCM encryption.

Use of Data

The information stored by the extension is used solely to autofill login credentials on cPanel accounts. No data is shared with third parties.

Security Measures

All passwords are encrypted locally before storage. The extension never sends passwords or other sensitive information over the internet.

User Rights

Users can delete all stored accounts at any time using the "Clear All" button in the extension.

Permissions Used

The extension requires the following permissions to function properly:

storage – To save and retrieve encrypted credentials locally.
tabs – To detect active tabs where the autofill should occur.
activeTab – To allow the extension to inject autofill scripts into the active cPanel page.
scripting – To execute scripts for autofilling credentials securely.
host_permissions () – To interact with all cPanel URLs entered by the user.
