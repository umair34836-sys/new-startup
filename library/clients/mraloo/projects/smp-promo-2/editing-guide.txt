 1. Overall Editing Rules
 • Dead air cut: Pauses, repeated words aur slow walking ke boring parts trim karo. Voice natural lage, robotic nahi.

 • Visual har 1–2 sec mein change: Gameplay angle, crop/zoom, subtitle emphasis, meme insert ya action shot. Har
 cut random nahi — narration se match hona chahiye.

 • Captions: Hinglish captions large, bold aur mobile-readable. Ek line mein ideally 3–6 words; important words ko alag
 colour/highlight karo. Captions face/UI ya IP ko cover na karein.

 • Sound balance: Voice sabse clear. Music low rakho; SFX short aur punchy. Dialogue ke important words par loud
 meme sound mat chalao.

 • Gameplay proof: Aloo SMP ke actual clips use karo. Features sirf tab dikhana jab server mein woh feature available
 ho.

 • No fake promises: 'Best server ever' ya 'thousands of players' tabhi bolna jab true ho. Real community moments
 zyada believable hain.

 Meme rule: meme insert 0.3–0.8 sec ka rakho. Overused meme clip ko full-screen lagane ke bajaye quick reaction
 zoom, subtitle ya sound cue use kar sakte ho. Copyrighted meme video/audio claim de sakta hai; licensed/original
 alternatives prefer karo.


 4. Captions & Visual Style
 • Font: bold, clean sans-serif (jaise Montserrat ExtraBold / Anton, agar available ho). White text + dark outline/shadow
 for readability.

 • Keyword highlight: “1000 PLAYERS”, “0 FRIENDS”, “ALOO SMP”, “JOIN NOW”. Har word ko rainbow colours mat do.

 • Safe area: subtitles centre-lower area mein, lekin bottom UI/buttons se upar. IP card ko centre mein high contrast ke
 saath rakho.

 • Pacing: 0–15 sec mein sabse zyada visual changes; server reveal ke baad gameplay ko itna time do ki viewers
 features samajh saken.

 • Transitions: mostly hard cuts. Random spin/glitch transitions har second mat use karo.


 5. Voiceover Direction — Natural Indian Creator Vibe
 • Voiceover ko ad jaisa mat padhna. Jaise friend ko Discord call par story bata rahe ho waise bolo.

 • “Bhai”, “yaar” natural jagah par; har line mein slang force mat karo.

 • Hook energetic ho, lekin shouting nahi. “ALOO SMP” reveal par excitement badhao.

 • Har sentence ke beech tiny natural pauses rakho; breaths ko completely remove mat karo.

 • Agar line boring lage, voice ko fast karne ke bajaye us line ke liye better gameplay shot choose karo.


 6. Export & Upload Checklist
 ■ Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920; export H.264 MP4. Source 60 FPS hai toh 60 FPS export; otherwise 30 FPS is fine.

 ■ Voice clear aur distortion-free. Headphones par check karo; phone speaker par bhi captions aur IP readable hone
 chahiye.

 ■ First 1 sec mein logo intro nahi. First frame se story/action start honi chahiye.

 ■ End screen par correct IP, port, supported edition (Java/Bedrock) aur Discord link agar relevant ho.

 ■ Video ko post karne se pehle ek baar bina sound dekho (captions clear?), aur ek baar screen dekhe bina suno (story
 samajh aati hai?).

 ■ Upload ke baad audience retention, average view duration, rewatches, shares aur comments check karo. Next edit
 mein data ke basis par hook improve karo.


 Final note
   Koi editor viral hone ki guarantee nahi de sakta. Is guide ka target hai: strong first 3–5 sec, relatable
   story, quick visual proof, clean audio, aur simple CTA. Clickbait ya fake player counts use na karein
   — actual server experience hi promote karein.
