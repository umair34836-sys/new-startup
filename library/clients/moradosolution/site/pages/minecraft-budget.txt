ANSWER UPDATED 4 OCTOBER 2026

What is MoradoSolution Minecraft hosting?

MoradoSolution Minecraft hosting is a managed Minecraft server service running on AMD EPYC 7C13 hardware that MoradoSolution owns and operates in Delhi, India. Every plan is provisioned instantly through a Pterodactyl control panel and includes NVMe storage, unmetered bandwidth, automated backups and in-house DDoS filtering backed by Cloudflare Magic Transit. Servers run on dedicated capacity with no swap memory, so tick rate stays stable when players load chunks, run modpacks or explore far from spawn. Java Edition, Bedrock Edition and modpacks such as Forge, Fabric, Paper and Purpur all run on the same plan, and billing is monthly in INR through UPI, net banking, cards and wallets with no forex charge. Plans start at ₹199 per month for 2 GB of RAM and scale to 64 GB for large modded networks, and upgrades are applied without reinstalling the server.

How much does Minecraft hosting cost in India?

MoradoSolution Minecraft hosting starts at ₹199 per month for 2 GB RAM and 6 GB NVMe storage. Mid-sized tiers run ₹299 to ₹1,599 per month, and large networks run ₹2,099 to ₹4,399 per month for 30 GB to 64 GB of RAM. Every plan includes DDoS protection, the Pterodactyl panel, automated backups and unlimited player slots, so the monthly price is the whole price.

Which plan should I choose?

Match RAM to the size of the server rather than player count alone. 2 GB suits a small vanilla world, 4 to 10 GB covers mid-sized SMPs and modpacks, 12 to 24 GB handles larger plugin and modded networks, and 30 GB and above is built for heavily modded servers and big player counts. Modded servers need roughly twice the RAM of a vanilla server at the same player count, so leave headroom before launching a large pack.

What is zero swap Minecraft hosting?

Your plan's RAM is reserved for your server alone, so the Java process never spills into disk backed swap memory. No swap thrashing, no sudden TPS drops, consistent tick times even at peak player hours.
