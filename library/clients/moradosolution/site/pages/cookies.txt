How MoradoSolution uses cookies, local storage, and tracking technologies.

1. Overview

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to provide functionality, analyze usage, and improve your experience. This policy explains what we use and why.

2. Types of Cookies
Essential Cookies: Required for website operation (session management, authentication, security)
Preferences Cookies: Remember your settings (theme, language, interface preferences)
Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site (anonymized)
Marketing Cookies: We do not use marketing or advertising cookies
3. Specific Cookies
Name	Type	Purpose	Duration
session_id	Essential	User session management	Session
csrf_token	Essential	CSRF protection	Session
theme	Preferences	Dark/light theme preference	1 year
_ga	Analytics	Google Analytics (anonymized)	2 years
4. Local Storage

We use browser localStorage for:

UI state persistence (sidebar open/closed, tab selections)
Form draft data (preventing loss on navigation)
No personal data is stored in localStorage
5. Third-Party Cookies

We do not allow third-party advertising networks to set cookies on our domains. Analytics providers (Google Analytics) may set cookies as described above.

6. Managing Cookies
Most browsers allow you to control cookies via settings
Disabling essential cookies will break website functionality
You can clear cookies at any time via browser settings
