ABUSE POLICY

Last Updated: 28 August 2026

MoradoSolution's zero-tolerance policy for abuse and abuse reporting procedures.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

MoradoSolution maintains a zero-tolerance policy for abuse. We take all abuse reports seriously and investigate promptly.

2. Types of Abuse

Abuse includes but is not limited to:

Network abuse: port scanning, brute-force attacks, exploit attempts, DDoS participation
Content abuse: illegal content, child exploitation material, extreme violence, hate speech
Resource abuse: sustained 100% CPU, disk I/O saturation, network saturation affecting other customers
Email abuse: spam, phishing, bulk unsolicited messages
Credential abuse: credential stuffing, account takeover attempts
3. Reporting Abuse

Abuse reports can be submitted via:

Email: abuse@moradosolution.com
Abuse reporting form (available in client area)
Support ticket with "Abuse Report" as subject

Please include relevant logs, timestamps, IP addresses, and any other evidence.

4. Investigation & Response

Abuse reports are investigated within 24 hours. Confirmed abuse may result in:

Immediate service suspension pending investigation
Notification to the customer
Permanent account termination for repeated or severe abuse (without refund)
Reporting to relevant authorities where legally required
5. False Reports

Knowingly submitting false abuse reports may result in account restrictions.
