What is MoradoSolution VPS hosting?

MoradoSolution VPS hosting provides KVM virtual private servers on AMD EPYC 7C13 processors with full root access and unmetered bandwidth, hosted in the company's own Delhi data centre. Instances are provisioned automatically and are reachable over SSH in under a minute, and every plan includes a dedicated IPv4 address, NVMe Gen4 storage and in-house DDoS filtering with Cloudflare Magic Transit at the network edge. Root access means any operating system image from Ubuntu, Debian, AlmaLinux or CentOS can be installed, along with Docker, Kubernetes, control panels or custom builds. Because CPU and disk capacity is dedicated rather than shared, sustained performance stays consistent under load instead of throttling on a noisy neighbour. Billing is monthly in INR through UPI, net banking, cards and wallets, with no forex charge on international cards.

How much does a VPS cost in India?

Eight plans run from ₹676 to ₹8,112 per month. Bandwidth is unmetered and DDoS protection is included on every plan, so the monthly price covers the full cost of the server. Every specification is listed in the plan table below.

Which VPS plan do I need?

Choose by workload rather than by price alone. Two vCPU and 8 GB RAM covers a small site, a proxy or a build runner. Six to ten vCPU with 16 GB to 24 GB RAM fits Docker stacks, databases and small production services. Thirty two vCPU with 96 GB RAM is sized for heavier compilation, video processing or multi service production workloads.

Every VPS plan includes unmetered bandwidth, one dedicated IPv4 address, full root access and DDoS protection.

Common questions.
What is the difference between VPS and VDS?

VPS (Virtual Private Server) shares physical hardware with other instances. VDS (Virtual Dedicated Server) guarantees dedicated CPU cores and resources. Both run on KVM with full root access.

Can I upgrade my VPS later?

Yes. You can scale up or down anytime from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately with prorated billing.

Do you support custom ISOs?

Yes. You can upload your own ISO through the panel and install any operating system you need.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, net banking, and all major credit/debit cards. All prices in INR.

Where are your servers located?

Our primary datacenter is in Delhi, India. Additional locations in Mumbai and Bangalore coming soon.
