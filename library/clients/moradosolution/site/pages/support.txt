03
FAQ

Answers, without the runaround.
How fast is my server set up?

Payment to playable usually takes minutes. Provisioning is automatic, and your panel login arrives by email as soon as installation finishes.

Is DDoS protection really included?

Yes — every plan, every location, no extra charge and nothing to configure. Filtering happens at the network level, ahead of your server.

Where are your servers located?

Across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. You pick the region at checkout, and we can move your server later if your community shifts.

Are backups included?

Backups are not automatic. You can create and manage your own server backups using Google Drive — you choose when to create a backup and keep control of your backup files.

Can you migrate my server from another host?

Yes. Send us your current host details and we move your files, configs, and databases free of charge — usually within a few hours, at a time that suits your players.

How does billing work?

Month-to-month through the panel, with no contracts or setup fees. Cancel whenever you like; service continues until the end of the paid period.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Upgrades apply in place without a reinstall — resources adjust after a short restart. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal.

How do I access the control panel?

Every plan ships with a browser-based panel including console, file manager, and task scheduler. Logins are emailed right after purchase, and you can add sub-users whenever.

What happens if I cancel?

Your server stays online until the end of the billing cycle, then gets archived for seven days in case you change your mind. After that, data is permanently deleted.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and select local payment methods shown at checkout. Annual billing is available on request.

Still have questions?

Open a ticket, join Discord, or email us. We are here to help.
