ANSWER UPDATED 4 OCTOBER 2026

What hardware runs MoradoSolution servers?

Every node in the MoradoSolution fleet runs on AMD EPYC 7C13 processors with DDR4 ECC memory and NVMe Gen 4 storage, connected over a 10 Gbps uplink and located in our own data centre in Delhi, India. Resources are dedicated rather than oversold, so the CPU and memory you are allocated are yours for as long as you hold the server. Mitigation is inline at the edge, which means attacks are filtered before they reach your node rather than after, and node telemetry watches hardware health continuously. We operate our own hardware and do not resell capacity from other providers, so the specification on this page is the specification your server actually runs on.

Why does dedicated hardware matter for Minecraft?

Minecraft is largely single thread bound, so per core clock speed and cache behaviour matter more than the raw core count. The EPYC platform provides both, and dedicated allocation means the clock behaviour you benchmark is the behaviour you get at peak hours. Full hardware and network detail is on the infrastructure page, and live node status is on the status page.
