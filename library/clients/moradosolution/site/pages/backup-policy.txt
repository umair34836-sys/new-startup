DATA & BACKUP POLICY

Last Updated: 28 August 2026

Backup responsibilities, data retention, and disaster recovery information.

1. Customer Responsibility

Customers are solely responsible for maintaining backups of their data. MoradoSolution does not guarantee backups unless explicitly included in the customer's specific service plan.

2. MoradoSolution Backup Services

Where backup services are provided (as part of specific plans or add-ons):

Backups are performed on a scheduled basis (typically daily)
Retention period varies by plan (typically 7-30 days)
Backups are stored in geographically separate locations where possible
Backup services are offered as a convenience and not a guarantee of data recovery
MoradoSolution is not responsible for backup failures, corruption, or incomplete backups
3. Data Loss Disclaimer

MoradoSolution is not responsible for data loss resulting from:

Customer actions, software failures, or accidental deletion
Hardware failures, maintenance, or service termination
Circumstances outside our reasonable control
Malware, ransomware, or security breaches on customer-managed systems
4. Best Practices

Customers should:

Maintain independent backups of important files and configurations
Test backup restoration procedures regularly
Store backups in multiple locations (including off-site)
Ensure data can be restored when required
Use version control for code and configuration files
5. Data Retention After Termination
Customer data may be permanently deleted 30 days after service cancellation or termination
Customers are responsible for downloading data prior to cancellation
No data recovery is possible after permanent deletion
Legal holds or regulatory requirements may extend retention periods
