FAIR USAGE POLICY

Last Updated: 28 August 2026

Fair usage guidelines for unlimited and unmetered service features.

1. Purpose

This Fair Usage Policy ensures that all customers receive consistent, reliable performance by preventing excessive consumption of shared resources.

2. Resource Fair Use

Unlimited or unmetered features are subject to reasonable fair-use requirements:

CPU: Sustained usage above 80% for extended periods may require upgrade
RAM: Consistent usage above 90% of allocation may trigger review
Storage: Excessive storage growth (e.g., logs, backups) may require cleanup or upgrade
Bandwidth: Sustained network saturation affecting other customers is not permitted
I/O: Disk I/O saturation degrading performance for other tenants is not permitted
3. Monitoring & Notification
Resource usage is monitored continuously
Customers approaching fair-use thresholds receive notification
Customers have 7 days to optimize or upgrade before restrictions may apply
Immediate restrictions may apply for severe impact on other customers
4. Restrictions

If fair-use thresholds are exceeded and not resolved:

MoradoSolution may implement throttling or quotas
Customers may be required to upgrade to a higher plan or dedicated resources
Persistent violations may result in service suspension
5. Exclusions

Fair-use policies do not apply to dedicated resource plans (dedicated CPU, RAM, storage) where resources are exclusively allocated.
