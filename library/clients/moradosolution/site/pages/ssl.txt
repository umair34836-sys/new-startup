01SSL Certificates

MoradoSolution provides SSL certificates (including Let's Encrypt integration) as a convenience for customers. Certificates may be automatically renewed where technically feasible.

MoradoSolution is not liable for certificate issuance failures or validation delays caused by upstream Certificate Authorities.
Customers may install custom SSL certificates where supported by the service plan.
SSL configuration must comply with current security best practices.
02Domain Security
Domain registration and management services are provided through third-party registrars.
MoradoSolution acts as a reseller and is not responsible for registrar policies, pricing changes, or disputes.
Customers are responsible for ensuring domain contact information is accurate.
Customers must renew domains before expiration to avoid service interruption.
Domain transfers follow registrar-specific procedures and timelines.
03Infrastructure Security
Traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ where applicable.
Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing algorithms (bcrypt/argon2).
Staff access to customer data is limited to what support genuinely requires and is logged.
MoradoSolution implements network-level DDoS mitigation, intrusion detection, and regular vulnerability scanning.
04Customer Security Responsibilities
Customers must maintain the security of their own applications, containers, and operating systems.
MoradoSolution is not liable for security breaches resulting from customer misconfiguration, unpatched software, or weak credentials.
Customers should enable two-factor authentication where available.
Regular security updates and patching are the customer's responsibility for self-managed services.
05Security Incidents

In the event of a security incident affecting customer data, MoradoSolution will notify affected customers without undue delay and take appropriate remedial action.
