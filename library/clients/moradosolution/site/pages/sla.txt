SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

Last Updated: 28 August 2026

MoradoSolution's service level commitments, uptime guarantees, and remediation policies.

1. Service Availability

MoradoSolution strives to maintain high-availability services. Specific uptime commitments vary by service plan:

Shared Hosting: 99.9% monthly uptime target
VPS/Dedicated: 99.95% monthly uptime target
Premium plans: 99.99% monthly uptime target

Uptime is measured at the network level and excludes scheduled maintenance, force majeure events, and issues caused by customer configuration.

2. Scheduled Maintenance
Announced at least 48 hours in advance via email and/or client area notifications
Typically performed during low-usage windows (02:00-06:00 UTC)
May include hardware upgrades, network improvements, security patches, or platform updates
Maintenance windows do not count against uptime calculations
3. Emergency Maintenance
Performed without advance notice to address critical security vulnerabilities, hardware failures, or imminent service degradation
Customers will be notified as soon as practicable via available channels
Duration is kept to the minimum necessary to resolve the issue
Emergency maintenance may count against uptime if exceeding 30 minutes
4. Service Credits

If uptime falls below the committed level for a given service plan, customers may be eligible for service credits:

99.9% target: 5% of monthly fee per 0.1% below target (max 50%)
99.95% target: 10% of monthly fee per 0.05% below target (max 50%)
99.99% target: 20% of monthly fee per 0.01% below target (max 50%)

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the affected month. Credits are applied as account credit, not cash refunds.

5. Exclusions

The SLA does not apply to:

Issues caused by customer configuration, applications, or scripts
Force majeure events (natural disasters, war, government action, etc.)
Upstream provider failures outside MoradoSolution's direct control
Scheduled maintenance windows
Free or trial services
6. Claims Process

To claim service credits, open a support ticket with "SLA Claim" as subject, including affected dates, times, and service identifiers. Claims are reviewed within 5 business days.
