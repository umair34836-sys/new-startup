Common questions.
How many players can each plan support?

Starter (2 GB) supports 5-10 players, Standard (6 GB) supports 20-30, Pro (12 GB) supports 70-100, Ultra (20 GB) supports 150-200.

Can I install Hytale mods?

Yes. All plans support Hytale mods and custom content with one-click install through the Pterodactyl panel.

Does Hytale support crossplay?

Yes. Hytale supports crossplay, and our servers come ready for it out of the box.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can scale up or down anytime from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately with prorated billing.

What hardware do you use?

AMD EPYC 7C13 processors (64 cores, 128 threads, Zen 3), DDR4 ECC memory, NVMe Gen4 SSDs, and 10 Gbps networking.

Where are your servers located?

Our primary datacenter is in Delhi, India. This provides 5-25ms ping to all major Indian cities.
