What is bare metal, and when do you need it?

Bare metal gives you an entire physical server with no hypervisor on top of it: no neighbour taking your CPU, no virtualization overhead, and disk I/O straight to the NVMe controller. Every machine is provisioned automatically and arrives with full root access.

Configurations, pricing and the workloads that justify the jump are covered in our bare metal guide, alongside the VDS, VPS and bare metal comparison.

Common questions.
What is a bare metal server?

A bare metal server is a physical server dedicated entirely to a single tenant. There is no virtualization layer or hypervisor — you have direct access to the full CPU, memory, storage, and network hardware.

Do you offer managed services?

Currently we offer unmanaged servers with full root access. Managed services and custom configurations are available on request — contact support for details.

What operating systems are supported?

Ubuntu, Debian, AlmaLinux, Rocky Linux, Fedora, CentOS, and custom ISOs. Select your OS during provisioning or reinstall anytime from the control panel.

What is the network speed?

All bare metal servers include unmetered bandwidth on 10 Gbps uplinks with Tier-1 Indian upstream providers. No traffic caps.

Where are the servers located?

Our primary datacenter is in Delhi, India. Low latency to Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad and all major Indian cities.

Can I upgrade from a VPS to bare metal?

Yes. You can migrate from any VPS or VDS plan to bare metal. Contact support to migrate with minimal downtime.
