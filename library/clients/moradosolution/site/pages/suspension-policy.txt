ACCOUNT SUSPENSION & TERMINATION POLICY

Last Updated: 28 August 2026

Conditions under which MoradoSolution may suspend or terminate customer accounts and services.

1. Grounds for Suspension

MoradoSolution may suspend services immediately for:

Non-payment or overdue invoices (after grace period)
Violation of the Acceptable Use Policy or Abuse Policy
Confirmed abuse reports (DDoS, malware, spam, etc.)
Security threats or unauthorized activity
Fraudulent or suspicious activity
Actions negatively affecting other customers or infrastructure
Legal orders or regulatory requirements
2. Suspension Process
Customers will be notified via email and/or client area
Suspension reason will be provided where legally permissible
Suspended services are inaccessible but data is preserved
Customers may appeal suspensions via support ticket
3. Reinstatement
Services may be reinstated upon resolution of the underlying issue
Payment of any applicable reinstatement fees may be required
Repeated suspensions may require additional verification or deposits
MoradoSolution reserves the right to deny reinstatement for severe violations
4. Grounds for Termination

MoradoSolution may terminate services immediately for:

Repeated or severe AUP/Abuse Policy violations
Fraud, identity theft, or payment fraud
Illegal activities hosted on or conducted through our services
Failure to resolve suspension issues within 30 days
Court orders or legal requirements
5. Termination Effects
All access to the service is permanently revoked
No refund is provided for the remainder of the billing period
Data may be permanently deleted after 30 days
Customers are responsible for downloading data prior to termination
Outstanding balances remain due and payable
6. Data After Termination

Customer data may be permanently deleted 30 days after termination. No data recovery is possible after permanent deletion. Legal holds may extend retention.
