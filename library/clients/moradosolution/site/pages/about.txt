THE COMPANY

MoradoSolution exists to host. We run game servers, VPS and dedicated infrastructure on hardware we operate ourselves — no resellers in the middle, no third-party cloud bolted on top.

As an independent provider we keep the team small, the answers direct, and the infrastructure honest. When something breaks, the people who reply are the people who run the network.

Game hosting and VPS run on the same AMD EPYC platform — dedicated vCPU, ECC memory and NVMe storage, deployed in minutes with automated setup.

Dedicated vCPU with sustained boost clocks across all plans.
