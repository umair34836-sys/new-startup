Who We Are

HostLixo provides Minecraft server hosting for players, friend groups, and growing communities. We make it easier to compare server plans, understand what resources are included, and find support and community links in one place.

01
Why We Started

Choosing a game server should not mean guessing what a plan includes. HostLixo focuses on clear plan information, familiar Minecraft software options, and a straightforward path from selecting a server to managing it.

Our Mission

Make Minecraft server hosting easier to understand with clear resources, practical tools, and helpful information for communities in India.

Our Vision

Help gaming communities choose a suitable setup, start their server, and find the information they need as their plans evolve.
