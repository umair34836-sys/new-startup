Common Questions
Which HostLixo Minecraft plan should I choose?
+

Compare nine AMD EPYC plans from 2 GB to 32 GB DDR5 ECC RAM. Check each plan's CPU allocation, NVMe storage, and included resources against your software, plugins, and expected player community.

What is included with each AMD EPYC plan?
+

Every listed plan includes its stated DDR5 ECC RAM, AMD EPYC CPU allocation and NVMe disk, two automated backup allocations, two extra ports, one MySQL database, and Enterprise L3/L4 DDoS protection.

How do I order a Minecraft server?
+

Open the Minecraft Plans page, choose a plan, and select Order on Discord. The HostLixo team can confirm the next steps there.

What Minecraft server software options are listed?
+

HostLixo lists Vanilla, Paper, Purpur, Spigot, Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge. Check the current panel options or ask the team if you need a specific version.

How many automated backups are included?
+

Every listed plan shows two automated backup allocations. For schedule and retention details, contact the team before ordering.

Does HostLixo include DDoS protection?
+

Every listed AMD EPYC plan includes Enterprise L3/L4 DDoS protection. See the Network & DDoS page for service details.

How can I contact HostLixo support?
+

Join the official Discord for community assistance and service announcements, or email support@hostlixo.in.
