## 3. Pakistan-specific findings

### Hooks, captions, language
- gameofbranding.com (May 16 2026, https://gameofbranding.com/instagram-reels-strategy-for-pakistani-brands/) says: hook
  in the first 1-3 s with one of three formulas (bold claim, curiosity gap, visual transformation); main text overlay
  within the first 2 s because "most Pakistani viewers watch Reels on mobile with their sound off in public spaces";
  turn on captions; use Urdu or Hinglish captions for food recipes.
- captionlagao.com (read Oct 24 2026, https://captionlagao.com/instagram-reels-captions-urdu): "Roman Urdu matches how
  people reply. Nastaliq is harder to read at Reel size unless the line is a title." Keep the bottom band free for the
  username/caption/buttons; centre is a safe default.
- Urdu-script fonts for video (urdu-nigaar.com, Aug 7 2026, https://www.urdu-nigaar.com/blog/urdu-fonts-for-capcut-editing/):
  Noto Nastaliq Urdu (clean subtitles), Jameel Noori Nastaleeq (poetry/quotes), Alvi Nastaleeq (dramatic titles), Mehr
  Nastaliq, Nafees Web Nastaleeq. It notes CapCut shows Urdu left-to-right by default unless an Urdu font is used.
  Noto Nastaliq Urdu is on Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Nastaliq+Urdu): the only one of
  these whose licence we could confirm (Google Fonts = OFL). The others: licence **unverified**.

### Audio and timing
- weproms.com (Aug 3 2026, https://weproms.com/blog/instagram-reels-audio-engagement-pakistan-beat-framework/) quotes
  Loopex Digital: Reels with trending audio get ~42% higher engagement; ZebraCat: 5.6% vs 3.1% engagement; NapoleonCat:
  ~22.4M Instagram users in Pakistan by Oct 2025. It advises a visual change every 3-5 s, cutting on the beat/drop, and
  posting 2-3 h before the evening peak (after Maghrib / post-Isha to midnight). These are the blog's numbers, not ours.
- gameofbranding.com (same page) gives different windows: 9-11 am, 1-2 pm, 8-11 pm PKT; fashion 9-11 pm. The two
  sources disagree on mornings; both agree on the evening.
- OUR LIMIT: trending in-app songs are copyrighted. Our music rule (CC0/PD/own only) means we deliver with no-credit
  music; whether Instagram's in-app music library can be added by the client after upload is **unverified** here.

### Wedding / event reels (Pakistan)
- barakaevents.com (Lahore planner, May 2026, https://www.barakaevents.com/blog/viral-pakistani-wedding-trends-2026):
  drone pull-back over the baraat as the opening shot; monochrome decor with one gold accent; couples want a
  **~90-second vertical** highlight film alongside the long film; AI-generated invitation teasers are common.
- hamzasproduction.com (Mar 13 2026, https://hamzasproduction.com/top-wedding-photography-trends-in-pakistan-2026-book-now/):
  candid/documentary coverage, film/analog-look grading (warm, soft highlights), moody dark grading for evening
  functions, golden hour, drone, same-day edit (2-4 min, shown at the reception/walima) as a requested add-on.
- aisle.studio (2026 playbook, https://aisle.studio/blog/how-to-edit-wedding-reels-for-instagram): 60-90 s; 3-second
  emotional hook (vow audio/first look); 30-45 cuts per minute (1.3-1.5 s per clip for a 60 s reel); duck music -9 dB
  under speech; names/date/venue lower-third at the END; export 1080x1920 H.264 12 Mbps 30 fps, AAC 192 kbps; it claims
  85% of plays are sound-off (their figure).
- frameflowedit.com (Mar 6 2026, https://frameflowedit.com/article/top-5-video-editing-trends-in-2026): vertical
  reframing as an editing job, AI-assisted grading, handheld/natural movement, FPV drone fly-throughs, phone + camera
  footage mixed in one timeline, documentary-cinematic hybrid pacing.

### Business / shop promo reels (Pakistan)
- Real estate (mediaintiation on steemit, Aug 2026, https://steemit.com/marketing/@mediaintiation/tutorial-short-form-video-editing-workflow-for-real-estate-tour-reels-in-pakistan):
  first 3 s = high-contrast text with size, exact sector/phase and price in PKR; fix wide-angle bowing; lift interior
  shadows; Urdu lapel voiceover over ambient bed at 15-20%; end with a permanent lower-third (phone, WhatsApp, location).
- Restaurants (loopverses.com, Jun 3 2026, https://www.loopverses.com/blog/how-pakistani-restaurants-cafes-get-more-customers-online):
  30-second kitchen clips (chef making the signature karahi) beat static photos. digigrow360.com (Feb 24 2026,
  https://digigrow360.com/instagram-menus-tiktok-food-videos-and-customer-engagement/): Lahore eateries use Story
  Highlights as menus with real, unedited dish videos.
- Fashion (gameofbranding.com, above): outfit transitions on trending audio, "one piece five ways", Eid/winter/festive
  moments, shoot BTS; it recommends 5+ Reels/week.
- Shop captions (sitey.pk prompt page, https://app.sitey.pk/ai-prompts/captions/): English + Roman Urdu captions with a
  clear price ("2 suits Rs 4,500"); the page claims priced captions get more messages (their claim).
- Eid: an Instagram creator reel captioned "Eid is incomplete without the transition videos" (#pakistan #karachi,
  https://www.instagram.com/anasilyas_k/reel/DH6hzfytdS2/). Viral Eid products were driven by reels in 2026: Kashmiri
