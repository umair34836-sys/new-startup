# Editing research notes

## Making a phone video look cinematic/romantic (web research, Oct 2026)
- Speed ramps: slow-mo on the emotional moment, normal/fast on setup; cut on the beat. (Premiere/IG tutorials)
- Vary clip speed and cut between similar frames for flow (r/Filmmakers).
- Warm interior grading: white-balance the scene first so skin is right, then mix warmth back in (r/colorists).
- Harsh window light: mask the subject / use depth to balance the window exposure (colour grading groups).
