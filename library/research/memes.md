## 1. How memes / meme SFX are used in desi vlog and reel editing

- Ducky Bhai, Maaz Safder and Sistrology use conversational Urdu with slang and English phrases; content mixes daily
  vlogs, challenges, travel and light humour, often with family and friends. [UMT Media and Communication Review,
  https://doi.org/10.32350/mcr.42.07, published 2024-12-26, checked 2026-10-25]
- Repo already records from the same study: "seamless and often humorous transitions, unexpected cuts, meme templates and
  playful sound effects (Ducky Bhai style)". [repo: library/research/editing/decision-guide.md section 29]
- A Hindi tutorial (YouTube AcRfyagshcY, 2026-06-25) says comedy Reels/vlogs "bhar-bhar ke" use funny sound effects and
  tells viewers to download them from the website "My Instants" (download MP3 button). [transcript via Exa,
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AcRfyagshcY, checked 2026-10-25] -> licence problem, see section 2.
- Seekho.in lesson "Viral Sound Effect Editing" also points to the My Instants website for funny/gaming sounds.
  [https://seekho.in/video/viral-sound-effect-editing/, checked 2026-10-25]
- A Hindi tutorial (YouTube K7KnoG8leRc) calls the short dialogue/exclamation clips dropped between vlog lines "meme
  sounds" and shows adding them in the VN editor (same method in CapCut). [transcript via Exa,
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K7KnoG8leRc, checked 2026-10-25]
- Skit tutorial advice: leave a pause after a line while talking so there is room to drop a meme sound/clip there.
  [transcript via Exa, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AQcNDlP1Tk4, 2026-05-20, checked 2026-10-25]
- CapCut's own sound library has a "meme effects" category beside transitions, whooshes, clicks, impact. [transcript via
  Exa, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UPNw3Psnp1g, checked 2026-10-25]
- Meme-video toolkit (ExpoCut guide): bold white caption with black outline at the top ("IMPACT" style), a zoom punch =
  two keyframes ending on the reaction, a sound effect starting on the visual hit, a freeze (3 s hold) on the
  punchline; "timing beats polish". [https://expocut.com/blog/make-a-meme-video.html, 2026-06-28, checked 2026-10-25]
- Comedy/meme edit list (iMovie series): jump cuts to remove pauses, zoom-ins on reactions, freeze frame on funny
  expressions, reverse/fast-forward, timed sound effects (whooshes, record scratches, air horns), on-screen text; keep
  meme videos 15-60 s. [https://yu-edtech.blogspot.com/2025/03/day-43-of-50-editing-comedy-or-meme.html, checked 2026-10-25]
- Zoom + whoosh: match the whoosh's peak to the peak of the zoom animation / transition. [transcript via Exa,
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WNFqIahxPIc, checked 2026-10-25]
- Where to use comedy SFX: sad trombone after a fail, crickets after a joke that didn't land / awkward silence, boing,
  bonk or cartoon fall on physical comedy and bloopers; use sparingly, a comedy sound on a serious point undercuts it.
  Start the sound on the first syllable of the word, keep effects 6-12 dB under the voice.
  [https://www.storycut.com/sound-effects/meme, checked 2026-10-25]
- Under speech a sting usually wants 60-80 % volume in CapCut; add a 0.1-0.2 s fade-out if it ends abruptly.
  [https://viralmemesfx.com/guides/meme-sound-effects-for-video-editing/, checked 2026-10-25 via Exa]
- Pakistani meme examples that went cross-border: Dananeer Mobin's "pawri ho rahi hai" clip (posted 6 Feb 2021) started
  a meme trend in India and Pakistan. [Deccan Chronicle,
  https://www.deccanchronicle.com/lifestyle/viral-and-trending/220221/vloggers-have-a-pawrrry.html, checked 2026-10-25]
  CapCut templates are built on such voice clips (e.g. "Nadia meri baggo ... ji" template). [https://capcuttemplate.io/nadia-jee-capcut-template/, 2025-10-12, checked 2026-10-25]
  -> these are a real person's voice/clip: see rules.

## 2. Copyright / licence situation

### YouTube mechanics
- Content ID claims can block, monetize (ads, sometimes sharing revenue) or track a video, per country. Shorts 1-3 min
  with an active claim are BLOCKED regardless of policy (no strike for a claim). Claims differ from strikes.
  [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6013276?hl=en, checked 2026-10-25]
- A claim "will keep you from monetizing the video, even if you only use a few seconds". Content ID can't decide fair
  use; disputes may go on to appeal and DMCA counter-notification. Credit lines like "I do not own" / "no infringement
  intended" are not fair use and not permission. Outside the US rules differ (EU categories; "fair dealing" elsewhere).
  [YouTube Help "Fair use on YouTube", https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9783148?hl=en, checked 2026-10-25]
- Content released under Creative Commons / open licences and public-domain recordings are NOT eligible as Content ID
  references. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2605065?hl=en, checked 2026-10-25 via Exa]
- Monetization "reused content": compilations/clips with little narrative, short videos compiled from other social media,
  content that gets views from mostly non-verbal reactions without voice commentary can be flagged; a "funny or thoughtful
  spin" that makes a meaningful difference is allowed. Applies to the channel as a whole.
  [YouTube Help community FAQ, https://support.google.com/youtube/community-guide/271248162, 2024-04-25; YouTube Help
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392, both checked 2026-10-25 via Exa]

### Meme sounds
- "Length does not protect you": US sound recordings are protected by originality, not duration (17 U.S.C. 102(a)(7));
  circuits split on tiny samples (Bridgeport 2005 vs VMG Salsoul 2016). [SoundboardOnline,
  https://soundboardonline.net/blog/are-meme-sounds-copyrighted, 2026-10-19, checked 2026-10-25]
- Same page: of its 1,384 hosted sounds, 207 are Freesound CC0; 1,173 are user uploads "mostly MyInstants" with "no
  licence grant", "treat as not cleared for monetized use". [same URL, checked 2026-10-25]
- myinstants.com/en/terms_of_use/ returned "Not Found" (agent-reach read, 2026-10-25): no licence for My Instants clips
  could be found.
- Vine Boom = Bluezone Corporation file Bluezone-Cimpact-sound-001.wav (pack released 7 Nov 2012). Bluezone grants a free
  worldwide licence incl. commercial and monetized Shorts/TikTok/Reels, credit not required, BUT forbids redistributing
  it: no resale, no inclusion "in a sound library", no offering it for direct download. Download link on the page.
  [Bluezone, https://www.bluezone-corporation.com/blog/original-vine-boom-sound-effect, published 2026-03-02, checked 2026-10-25]
- Roblox "oof": ownership dispute, removed 26 Jul 2022, back as default death sound 31 Jul 2025. "Fahhh": a living
  streamer's voice; publicity rights may apply. [SoundboardOnline URL above, checked 2026-10-25]
- Popular meme sounds often come from TV, films, games or songs and can be muted or claimed; a third party sometimes puts
  a CC0 sound inside a registered track, causing a mistaken claim: dispute and point to the original Freesound page.
  [https://www.storycut.com/sound-effects/meme, checked 2026-10-25]

### Meme clips / images (India + Pakistan)
- India: Delhi High Court (Aug 2024) restrained unauthorised use of Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah characters/content;
  meme pages "routinely use clips from popular shows". [Mint,
  https://www.livemint.com/industry/indias-meme-marketing-boom-faces-legal-reckoning-as-copyright-battles-heat-up-11747370957853.html, 2025-05-18, checked 2026-10-25]
- India: ANI copyright strikes on creators for short news clips (Mohak Mangal: strikes incl. a 9-second clip; ANI asked
  for subscriptions/fees). Three strikes in 90 days -> channel terminated. [Mint,
  https://www.livemint.com/technology/youtubers-ani-mohak-mangal-copyright-subscriptions-youtube-video-services-content-creators-ashwini-vaishnaw-laws-hc-11748534949527.html, 2025-05-30;
  The Reporters' Collective, https://www.reporters-collective.in/trc/youtube-says-it-doesnt-judge-copyright-violation-we-found-it-does, 2025-05-27; both checked 2026-10-25]
- A meme-reaction channel (21k subs) got 2 strikes on 3-year-old videos of viral student clips. [Reddit r/PartneredYoutube,
  https://www.reddit.com/r/PartneredYoutube/comments/1nefr7n/, 2025-09-11, checked 2026-10-25] (one user's report)
- India's fair dealing (s.52 Copyright Act 1957) has no standalone parody exception; it covers criticism and review.
  [Zenodo chapter, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22986015, 2026-09-27; SpicyIP,
  https://spicyip.com/2025/07/exploring-the-parody-satire-distinction-in-light-of-the-kunal-kamra-saga.html, both checked 2026-10-25]
- Photos used in memes are "artistic works" (s.2(c)); Getty made a blog pay for "Socially Awkward Penguin" use.
  [Legal 500, https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/intellectual-property/copyright-law-%26-internet-memes, 2022-12-09, checked 2026-10-25]
- Pakistan: Copyright Ordinance 1962 s.57(1)(a): fair dealing with a literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work for (i)
  research or private study, (ii) criticism or review. [WIPO Lex, https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/3482,
  checked 2026-10-25 via Exa excerpt; full text https://fia.gov.pk/files/act/27.pdf]

## Practical rules for our edits (only from the facts above)

1. Never use My Instants / soundboard rips, TV/film/drama clips, songs or a real person's viral voice clip in a client
   or monetized video: no licence found, claims block 1-3 min Shorts, strikes can end a channel (sections 2).
2. Meme sounds: use `library/memes/sfx/` + the repo ids in section 4. CC0 sounds can't be Content ID references, so a
   claim on them is a mistake to dispute with the Freesound page link.
3. Vine Boom is allowed IN a video (Bluezone licence, no credit needed) but must NOT be committed to `library/`
   (licence forbids sound libraries/redistribution): fetch it from Bluezone's page into the scratchpad per job, or use
   `impact-boom` / `sub-drop`.
4. Recreate meme FORMATS with own assets: bold outlined caption, zoom punch on the reaction, SFX on the visual hit,
   freeze on the punchline, crickets on an awkward silence, sad trombone on a fail. Leave a pause in the VO for each.
5. Mix: SFX start on the first syllable / peak on the cut, 6-12 dB under the voice; short fade-out.
6. Comedy SFX sparingly; not on serious lines (client offers, prices).
7. For monetized channels add real voice commentary / story; pure clip compilations or silent reactions risk "reused
   content".
8. Reaction GFX: AssetPolish CC0 animated emoji stickers or own drawn ones; no celebrity/drama screenshots (India photos =
   artistic works; Pakistan/India fair dealing = criticism/review, no parody exception in India).
