# Audio mix and master for video
Scope: mixing and mastering for video - gain staging, buses, compression, multiband, limiting, sidechain ducking, stereo imaging, panning, reverb, loudness specs per platform/broadcast, true peak, metering, mono compatibility (not dialogue repair).
Researched 2026-10-24 via agent-reach (Exa) web search, official docs (ffmpeg, EBU, ATSC, AES, Spotify, Netflix) and local tests with ffmpeg 6.1.1 on synthetic clips.


## Meter checks: prove the meter before trusting it
1761. **Read -18 LUFS from the -18 dBFS alignment tone** — EBU Tech 3341 says a 1 kHz stereo sine, in phase, peaking at -18 dBFS must read -18.0 LUFS; use it to check any meter chain. **How:** `ffmpeg -f lavfi -i "aevalsrc=exprs=0.125893*sin(2*PI*1000*t)|0.125893*sin(2*PI*1000*t):s=48000:d=20" -af ebur128=peak=true+sample:framelog=quiet -f null -` read I -18.0 LUFS, peak -18.0 dBFS (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3341 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1762. **EBU Mode test case 1 (-23 dBFS tone)** — Tech 3341 Table 1 case 1: 20 s stereo 1 kHz at -23 dBFS per channel must read M, S, I = -23.0 ±0.1 LUFS. **How:** same command with amplitude `0.070795` read I -23.0 LUFS (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3341 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1763. **EBU Mode gating test (case 5)** — Tech 3341 case 5: 20 s at -26 dBFS, 20.1 s at -20 dBFS, 20 s at -26 dBFS must integrate to -23.0 ±0.1 LUFS; it checks the relative gate. **How:** `aevalsrc=exprs=if(between(t\,20\,40.1)\,0.1\,0.050119)*sin(2*PI*1000*t)|...:d=60.1` into `ebur128` read I -23.0 LUFS (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3341 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1764. **LRA test signals (Tech 3342)** — Tech 3342 cases: 20 s at -20 dBFS then 20 s at -30 dBFS must give LRA 10 ±1 LU; -40/-20 must give 20 ±1 LU. **How:** two-step `aevalsrc` tones (`if(lt(t\,20)\,0.1\,0.031623)` etc.) into `ebur128` read LRA 10.0 LU and 20.0 LU (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3342 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3342.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1765. **Mono files read 3 dB low unless dual-mono** — ebur128 and loudnorm have a dualmono option because a mono file meant for stereo playback measures perceptually wrong; ebur128's panlaw defaults to -3.01 dB. **How:** a mono 1 kHz tone at -18 dBFS read I -21.0 LUFS plain and -18.0 LUFS with `ebur128=framelog=quiet:dualmono=true` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#loudnorm, checked 2026-10-24]
1766. **ffmpeg's sine source is -18 dBFS by default** — Know your generator level before using it for alignment. **How:** `ffmpeg -f lavfi -i sine=f=1000:d=2 -af astats=measure_perchannel=none:measure_overall=Peak_level -f null -` read peak -18.06 dB (tested here 2026-10-24) [local test, ffmpeg 6.1.1, 2026-10-24]
1767. **LFE is left out of loudness** — BS.1770 excludes the LFE channel of 5.1 from the loudness measurement (Tech 3343 §4.2); A/85 short-form measurement also excludes LFE. **How:** a 5.1 file with signal only in LFE read I -70.0 LUFS (nothing counted) in `ebur128` (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3343 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3343.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ATSC A/85:2013 with Corrigendum 1, https://www.atsc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/A85-2013-with-Corrigendum-No-1.pdf, checked 2026-10-24]
1768. **Surround channels weigh +1.5 dB** — BS.1770 weights the surround pair +1.5 dB, while the default downmix coefficient for them is -3 dB, a 4.5 dB loudness difference (Tech 3343 §7.1). **How:** the same -20 dBFS tone read -23.0 LUFS in FL and -21.5 LUFS in a surround channel of a 5.1 file (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3343 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3343.pdf, checked 2026-10-24]
1769. **Silence barely moves Integrated, but LRA jumps** — Integrated loudness gates at -70 LUFS absolute and -10 LU relative; Tech 3342 warns that leading/trailing silence in short items can give misleadingly high LRA. **How:** adding 10 s of silence with `apad=pad_dur=10` moved I only -15.4 to -15.5 LUFS but LRA went 0.0 to 7.0 LU (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3341 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [EBU Tech 3342 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3342.pdf, checked 2026-10-24]
1770. **Know the three EBU time scales** — Momentary = 400 ms sliding window, Short-term = 3 s, Integrated = start to stop with the BS.1770 gate; no extra meter ballistics in EBU Mode. **How:** read M/S/I from `ebur128` (it logs at 10 Hz); `framelog=quiet` keeps only the summary (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3341 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1771. **Relative gate is -10 LU, older meters used -8** — R 128 revision August 2011 changed the relative gate from -8 to -10 LU; a meter on the old gate reads differently. **How:** check the meter's version before comparing numbers; ffmpeg `ebur128` prints its two thresholds (I threshold = I - 10 LU) [EBU R 128 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/r/r128.pdf, checked 2026-10-24]
1772. **EBU +9 and +18 scales** — EBU Mode meters offer +9 (-18 to +9 LU) by default and +18 (-36 to +18 LU) for wide-range programmes; 0 LU = -23 LUFS. **How:** `ebur128=video=1:meter=18` draws the +18 scale (rendered here to a 640x480 graph video) (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3341 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1773. **Set the graph target for online work** — ebur128's target option sets the 0 LU point of the visual meter; the docs note online material may prefer -16 LUFS. **How:** `-filter_complex "ebur128=video=1:meter=18:target=-16[v][a]" -map [v] -map [a]` made a graph video (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1774. **Short-term gauge and LU scale** — gauge=shortterm shows short-term instead of momentary in the gauge (useful for live mixing); scale=relative shows LU against target. **How:** `ebur128=video=1:gauge=shortterm:scale=relative` rendered fine (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1775. **LRA needs about a minute** — R 128 does not recommend LRA for programmes shorter than 1 minute; Tech 3341 says the first 60 s of LRA must be flagged as not yet stable. **How:** for a reel, judge Integrated, max short-term and true peak; ignore LRA [EBU R 128 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/r/r128.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [EBU Tech 3341 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf, checked 2026-10-24]
1776. **LRA = 10th to 95th percentile** — LRA uses 3 s short-term values, gated at -70 LUFS and -20 LU relative, and takes the spread from the 10th to the 95th percentile, so one gunshot or a fade-out cannot set it. **How:** read `LRA low` and `LRA high` in the `ebur128` summary to see the two percentiles (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3342 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3342.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1777. **Dump short-term loudness per 100 ms** — ebur128 metadata=1 cuts audio into 100 ms frames carrying lavfi.r128.* keys; print them to find the loudest 3 s. **How:** `ebur128=metadata=1,ametadata=mode=print:key=lavfi.r128.S:file=st.txt` wrote 60 values for 6 s; max S was -15.349 LUFS (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1778. **Peak-to-Loudness Ratio** — PLR = maximum true peak minus programme loudness, a measure of micro-dynamics (R 128 s2 glossary). **How:** `ebur128=peak=true` on the test bed: TP -9.4 dBTP, I -15.4 LUFS, PLR 6.0 dB (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU R 128 s2 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/r/r128s2.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [Euphona, https://euphonaai.com/tools/loudness-checker, checked 2026-10-24]
1779. **True peak needs an oversampling meter** — Tech 3343: a sample-peak meter cannot predict overs from sample-rate conversion or codecs; a 4x true-peak meter can under-read ~0.5 dB at 48 kHz, so leave 1 dB. **How:** always add `peak=true` to `ebur128` (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3343 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3343.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24]
1780. **Make an inter-sample-peak test file** — A full-scale 12 kHz sine at 45 deg phase has samples at -3 dBFS but a reconstructed peak above 0 dBFS. **How:** `aevalsrc=exprs=sin(2*PI*12000*t+PI/4)|...:s=48000` saved as float WAV read sample peak -3.0 dBFS, true peak +0.6 dBTP (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128, checked 2026-10-24] [FabFilter Pro-L 2 help, https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-l/using/outputoptions, checked 2026-10-24]
1781. **Use a peak meter for alignment, not a loudness meter** — Tech 3343 §8.1: K-weighting makes loudness meters error-prone for alignment; EBU recommends a peak meter and 1 kHz at -18 dBFS. **How:** check the tone with `astats=measure_perchannel=none:measure_overall=Peak_level` (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3343 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3343.pdf, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#astats, checked 2026-10-24]

## astats, volumedetect and other level meters
1782. **astats overall only** — measure_perchannel=none plus a short measure_overall list keeps astats output to the numbers you need. **How:** `astats=measure_perchannel=none:measure_overall=Peak_level+RMS_level+Crest_factor` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#astats, checked 2026-10-24]
1783. **Spot clipping with Flat_factor and Peak_count** — Flat_factor is the run of identical samples at the peak; Peak_count counts times the signal hit min/max. **How:** the clean bed: Flat factor 0, Peak count 2; the same bed pushed +15 dB into s16 WAV: Flat factor 34.3, Peak count 69190 (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#astats, checked 2026-10-24]
1784. **Find DC offset** — astats DC_offset shows mean displacement; dcshift docs say DC offset reduces headroom. **How:** `dcshift=shift=0.05` gave DC offset 0.0499; adding `highpass=f=10` brought it to -0.00004 (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#astats, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#dcshift, checked 2026-10-24]
1785. **Per-half-second RMS log** — astats metadata=1 with reset=1 writes lavfi.astats.* keys per frame; set frame size with asetnsamples. **How:** `asetnsamples=24000,astats=metadata=1:reset=1:measure_perchannel=none:measure_overall=RMS_level,ametadata=mode=print:key=lavfi.astats.Overall.RMS_level:file=rms.txt` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#astats, checked 2026-10-24]
1786. **RMS peak and trough window** — astats length sets the short window (default 50 ms) used for RMS_peak/RMS_trough. **How:** `astats=length=0.4:measure_overall=none:measure_perchannel=RMS_peak+RMS_trough` gave -17.07 / -19.11 dB (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#astats, checked 2026-10-24]
1787. **Crest factor per channel** — Crest_factor is peak/RMS (not in dB) - low numbers mean squashed audio. **How:** `astats=measure_overall=none:measure_perchannel=Crest_factor+Dynamic_range+Noise_floor` gave 1.92 and 2.41 on the two channels (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#astats, checked 2026-10-24]
1788. **volumedetect histogram = safe boost** — volumedetect reports mean/max and a histogram; per the docs, if max is -4 dB you can raise +4 dB without clipping. **How:** `-af volumedetect`: mean -17.1 dB, max -9.5 dB, so +9 dB is the sample-peak limit (true peak still to check) (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#volumedetect, checked 2026-10-24]
1789. **ReplayGain scan** — replaygain prints track_gain and track_peak at the end and passes audio unchanged. **How:** `-af replaygain` printed track_gain +1.92 dB, track_peak 0.336 (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#replaygain, checked 2026-10-24]
1790. **Phase value per frame** — aphasemeter exports lavfi.aphasemeter.phase in [-1, 1]: -1 out of phase, 1 in phase. **How:** `aphasemeter=video=0,ametadata=mode=print:key=lavfi.aphasemeter.phase:file=ph.txt` (bed read 0.48) (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#aphasemeter, checked 2026-10-24]
1791. **Auto-detect mono stretches** — aphasemeter phasing=1 logs mono and out-of-phase sequences longer than duration. **How:** `aphasemeter=video=0:phasing=1:duration=1:tolerance=0.001` on a dual-mono file logged mono_start 0 / mono_end 6 (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#aphasemeter, checked 2026-10-24]
1792. **Auto-detect out-of-phase stretches** — angle (default 170 deg) sets the out-of-phase threshold. **How:** L = sine, R = -sine into `aphasemeter=video=0:phasing=1:duration=1:angle=170` logged out_phase_start 0 / end 5 (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#aphasemeter, checked 2026-10-24]
1793. **Read a correlation meter** — +1 = dual mono, 0 = fully decorrelated/wide, steady readings below 0 = reduced mono compatibility; meters integrate ~600 ms. **How:** use the aphasemeter phase value as the correlation reading [Sound On Sound, https://www.soundonsound.com/sound-advice/q-what-are-my-phase-correlation-meters-telling-me, checked 2026-10-24]
1794. **Vectorscope video** — avectorscope draws L/R as a Lissajous figure: vertical line = mono, horizontal = out of phase. **How:** `avectorscope=s=360x360:draw=line:scale=sqrt:zoom=1.5` rendered to MP4 (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#avectorscope, checked 2026-10-24]
1795. **Burn a level meter onto a QA copy** — showvolume turns levels into video; m=r for RMS, dm shows a max line. **How:** `[0:a]showvolume=r=30:w=320:h=12:m=r:dm=1[vol];[0:v][vol]overlay=0:H-h` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#showvolume, checked 2026-10-24]
1796. **Windowed L/R correlation as audio** — axcorrelate outputs a -1..1 signal per window; the docs example splits a stereo file first. **How:** `channelsplit,axcorrelate=size=1024:algo=fast` then astats (min -0.80 on the bed) (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#axcorrelate, checked 2026-10-24]
1797. **Spectrum picture of the mix** — showspectrumpic renders one image of the whole spectrum; mode=separate shows channels apart. **How:** `-lavfi showspectrumpic=s=800x400:mode=separate:color=intensity -frames:v 1 spec.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#showspectrumpic, checked 2026-10-24]
1798. **Waveform picture** — showwavespic renders one waveform image; split_channels and scale=log help read quiet parts. **How:** `-lavfi showwavespic=s=800x200:split_channels=1:scale=log -frames:v 1 wav.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#showwavespic, checked 2026-10-24]
1799. **Level histogram video** — ahistogram shows the volume histogram, separate per channel with dmode=separate. **How:** `ahistogram=dmode=separate:s=640x360` rendered (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ahistogram, checked 2026-10-24]
1800. **drmeter numbers** — drmeter: DR 14+ very dynamic, 8-13 transition, under 8 very compressed (3 s windows by default). **How:** `-af drmeter` ran but printed 'No data, dynamic range not meassurable' on these 6-36 s test beds in ffmpeg 6.1; don't rely on it until checked on real music [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#drmeter, checked 2026-10-24]

## Gain staging and headroom
1801. **Alignment level stays -18 dBFS** — Tech 3343 §8.1: the 1 kHz alignment tone for programme exchange stays at -18 dBFS; it reads -18 LUFS on a loudness meter if in both channels. **How:** make it with `aevalsrc=exprs=0.125893*sin(2*PI*1000*t)|0.125893*sin(2*PI*1000*t)` (tested here 2026-10-24) [EBU Tech 3343 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3343.pdf, checked 2026-10-24]
1802. **A/85 -20 dBFS test tones** — A/85 annex test signals: 1 kHz sine at -20 dBFS and 500 Hz-2 kHz band-limited pink noise at -20 dBFS for monitor setup. **How:** `aevalsrc=exprs=0.1*sin(2*PI*1000*t)` peak read -20 dBFS (tested here 2026-10-24) [ATSC A/85:2013 with Corrigendum 1, https://www.atsc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/A85-2013-with-Corrigendum-No-1.pdf, checked 2026-10-24]
1803. **Gain staging targets** — Sauce Mastering: mix peaks around -6 dBFS (-6 to -3) with nothing clipping; The Audio Owl: many set levels between -12 and -20 dBFS. **How:** trim each stem with `volume=-6dB` etc. and check `astats` Peak_level (tested here 2026-10-24) [Sauce Mastering, https://saucemastering.com/blog/gain-staging-and-headroom, checked 2026-10-24] [The Audio Owl, https://theaudioowl.com/mixing/what-is-gain-staging/, checked 2026-10-24]
1804. **Write gains in dB** — volume accepts dB: -6.0206dB halves the level (docs example). **How:** `volume=-6.0206dB` took the bed peak from -9.5 to -15.5 dB (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#volume, checked 2026-10-24]
1805. **dB to HyperFrames data-volume** — data-volume and filter gains are linear; 10^(dB/20). **How:** `python3 -c "print([round(10**(d/20),3) for d in (-3,-6,-10,-12,-18,-20)])"` -> 0.708 0.501 0.316 0.251 0.126 0.1 (tested here 2026-10-24) [local test, ffmpeg 6.1.1, 2026-10-24]
1806. **amix divides by the number of inputs** — amix 'normalize' (default on) scales inputs instead of only summing; turn it off when you set levels yourself. **How:** voice+music: default amix RMS -20.9 dB, `amix=normalize=0` -14.9 dB (6 dB apart with 2 inputs) (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#amix, checked 2026-10-24]
1807. **amix weights as faders** — weights sets each input's gain (docs example: vocal 1, music 0.25, normalize=0). **How:** `amix=inputs=2:weights='1 0.25':normalize=0:duration=first:dropout_transition=0` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#amix, checked 2026-10-24]
1808. **Stop the level jump when a stem ends** — When an input ends, amix renormalizes over dropout_transition (default 2 s); with normalize=0 and dropout_transition=0 nothing rescales. **How:** `[v][1]amix=dropout_transition=0:normalize=0` with a voice that ends at 3 s (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#amix, checked 2026-10-24]
1809. **End the mix with the picture/voice** — amix duration=first stops at the first input's length (longest is default). **How:** put the voice or a picture-length bed first and use `duration=first` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#amix, checked 2026-10-24]
1810. **Bus graph with stem outputs** — iZotope's video mix layout: group dialogue, B-roll, ambience, SFX, music into submasters so each gets bus processing. **How:** tested command (one graph, four outputs): `[D1][M1][E1]amix=inputs=3:normalize=0[mix]` with `asplit` copies mapped to dial.wav, mus.wav, fx.wav (tested here 2026-10-24) [iZotope, https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/mixing-audio-for-video-part-4-mixing-techniques, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#asplit, checked 2026-10-24]
1811. **Deliver stems and M&E** — Producers may ask for an M&E (everything but dialogue) and separate D/M/E stems; iZotope keeps a template with print tracks for all of them. **How:** print `[M][E]amix=normalize=0` as the M&E plus each stem from the same graph [iZotope, https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/mixing-audio-for-video-part-4-mixing-techniques, checked 2026-10-24]
1812. **Pan '<' renormalises** — In pan, replacing '=' with '<' renormalises gains to a total of 1, avoiding clipping. **How:** stereo to mono: `pan=mono|c0=c0+c1` peak -5.6 dB, `pan=mono|c0<c0+c1` peak -11.6 dB (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#pan, checked 2026-10-24]
1813. **Null test two renders** — Invert one copy (aeval -val) and sum with amix normalize=0: identical files cancel. **How:** `[1]aeval=-val(ch):c=same[i];[0][i]amix=normalize=0,astats=...Peak_level` read -inf for identical files, -16.2 dB RMS for a different one (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#aeval, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#amix, checked 2026-10-24]
1814. **Fixed-point volume** — precision=fixed limits formats to U8/S16/S32 (docs example: 6dB fixed). **How:** `volume=volume=6dB:precision=fixed` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#volume, checked 2026-10-24]
1815. **Float the whole graph** — amix only works in float and auto-inserts aresample for integer inputs; aformat can set the format explicitly. **How:** `[0]aformat=sample_fmts=fltp:sample_rates=48000:channel_layouts=stereo[a];[a][1]amix=normalize=0` then `-c:a pcm_f32le` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#amix, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#aformat, checked 2026-10-24]
1816. **Set foreground levels a little low** — Tech 3343 §3.2: background sounds add to programme loudness, so set foreground (narrator) slightly below target and rise later if needed. **How:** set the voice bus, then check `ebur128` I before adding beds [EBU Tech 3343 (2023), https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3343.pdf, checked 2026-10-24]

## Normalising: loudnorm and static gain

## Reverb, delay and space
1954. **Reverb on a send, 100% wet** — SOS: a wet/dry knob on an insert turns the dry sound down as you add reverb; LordReverb: sends at 100% wet so all tracks share one space. **How:** tested command: `asplit[d][s]` -> wet chain -> `[d][w]amix=weights='1 0.25':normalize=0` (tested here 2026-10-24) [Sound On Sound, https://www.soundonsound.com/sound-advice/q-why-should-use-reverb-send-effect, checked 2026-10-24] [LordReverb, https://lordreverb.com/mixing/reverb-delay-mixing-guide/, checked 2026-10-24]
1955. **Abbey Road reverb filters** — SOS: high-pass the reverb ~200 Hz, 12-18 dB/oct, and low-pass ~10 kHz. **How:** `highpass=f=200,lowpass=f=10000` on the send (tested here 2026-10-24) [Sound On Sound, https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/how-optimise-your-reverb-treatments, checked 2026-10-24]
1956. **Pre-delay by source** — iZotope: pre-delay 20-80 ms on vocals, 40-100 ms on guitar, 5-50 ms on drums. **How:** `adelay=25:all=1` before the reverb on the send (tested here 2026-10-24) [iZotope, https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/reverb-pre-delay, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#adelay, checked 2026-10-24]
1957. **Filtered echoes into reverb** — SOS: a repeating delay with lows rolled off ~300 Hz and highs above ~1.2 kHz recedes nicely. **How:** tested command: `aecho=0.8:0.9:300|600:0.4|0.3,highpass=f=300,lowpass=f=1200` on a send (tested here 2026-10-24) [Sound On Sound, https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/how-optimise-your-reverb-treatments, checked 2026-10-24]
1958. **aecho doubling** — Docs: aecho=0.8:0.88:60:0.4 sounds like twice as many instruments. **How:** `aecho=0.8:0.88:60:0.4` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#aecho, checked 2026-10-24]
1959. **aecho mountains** — Docs: 1000 ms (and 1800 ms) delays sound like open-air mountains; 6 ms sounds metallic/robot. **How:** `aecho=0.8:0.9:1000|1800:0.3|0.25` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#aecho, checked 2026-10-24]
1960. **Convolution reverb with afir** — afir convolves the first input with an IR stream (docs: mono IR as second input). **How:** `ffmpeg -i voice.wav -i ir.wav -lavfi afir conv.wav` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#afir, checked 2026-10-24]
1961. **Synthetic IR for tests** — No real IR here; a decaying noise burst works as a test IR. **How:** `anoisesrc=c=white:a=0.5:r=48000:d=1.6:s=7` + `afade=t=out:st=0:d=1.6:curve=exp,lowpass=f=8000` -> ir.wav (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#anoisesrc, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#afade, checked 2026-10-24]
1962. **afir dry/wet** — dry sets input gain, wet the output gain. **How:** `afir=dry=10:wet=10` inside the send graph (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#afir, checked 2026-10-24]
1963. **Switch IRs** — nbirs inputs switchable with ir (runtime command). **How:** `[s][i0][i1]afir=nbirs=2:ir=1` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#afir, checked 2026-10-24]

1965. **IR length limit** — maxir default 30 s, range 0.1-60 s. **How:** `afir=maxir=60` for long halls (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#afir, checked 2026-10-24]
1966. **Chorus** — Docs: typical delays 40-60 ms; two-delay example. **How:** `chorus=0.6:0.9:50|60:0.4|0.32:0.25|0.4:2|1.3` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#chorus, checked 2026-10-24]
1967. **Flanger** — delay 0-30 ms, depth 0-10 ms, regen -95..95%. **How:** `flanger=delay=2:depth=3:regen=20:speed=0.3` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#flanger, checked 2026-10-24]
1968. **Tremolo and ring mod** — tremolo f <= 20 Hz is tremolo; higher acts as ring modulator. **How:** `tremolo=f=4.8:d=0.4` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#tremolo, checked 2026-10-24]
