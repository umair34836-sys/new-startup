## YouTube specs, Studio and API
4161. **Studio upload vs Get suggestions** — under "Thumbnail" in Studio you either Upload file or click Get suggestions to have one generated; custom upload needs a verified account. **How:** Studio > Content > video > Thumbnail > Upload file; hand the owner our JPG for this step. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4162. **Shorts custom thumbnail: computer only** — custom Shorts thumbnails can currently only be added in YouTube Studio on a computer (Content > Shorts tab), recommended 9:16. **How:** tell the owner to set it from a desktop browser, not the phone app; export 2160x3840 or 1080x1920. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4163. **Upload size limit depends on device** — mobile 2 MB for video thumbnails (10 MB for podcasts), desktop 50 MB for video, Shorts and podcast thumbnails. **How:** if the owner uploads from the phone, deliver a JPG under 2 MB; from desktop a big PNG is fine. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4164. **Podcast playlists are 1:1** — podcast playlist thumbnails should be 1:1, not 16:9. **How:** render a separate square cover for podcast playlists, e.g. `ImageOps.pad(im,(1080,1080))` (see PIL section). [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4165. **Vertical video + 16:9 thumbnail gets replaced on Home** — a vertical video with a 16:9 custom thumbnail is shown with an auto-generated 4:5 thumbnail on Home, Explore and Subscriptions; the custom one still shows on the watch feed, history and non-mobile. **How:** for vertical uploads make the custom thumbnail 9:16 so YouTube has no reason to swap it. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4166. **Daily custom thumbnail limit** — channels have a daily upload cap ("Daily custom thumbnail limit reached": try again in 24 h); limits vary by country and channel history, and strikes reduce it. **How:** finalise A/B variants before uploading; don't upload many test versions in one day. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4167. **Strikes for thumbnails** — thumbnails with nudity/sexual content, hate speech, violence or harmful content can be rejected and bring a strike; repeat offences can remove custom-thumbnail rights for 30 days. **How:** check every FireCraft/PvP thumbnail for gore before export. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4168. **Custom thumbnails can be hidden in search** — YouTube may turn off custom thumbnails in certain search results when considered inappropriate. **How:** keep a clean version; if impressions in Search drop, check the thumbnail first. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4169. **Ask Studio thumbnail ideas** — Ask Studio analyses the transcript and themes to suggest thumbnails; prompt "Create a thumbnail", add the video or reference images, refine with follow-ups (e.g. "make the text larger"). **How:** owner-side tool in the publishing flow; use our render as reference image if wanted. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4170. **Misleading thumbnail = policy violation** — the thumbnails policy bans a thumbnail that makes viewers think they'll see something not in the video, plus vulgar text, gore and sexual imagery. **How:** only show moments that are in the video. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229980?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4171. **Age-restriction instead of strike** — a borderline thumbnail may get the video age-restricted or the thumbnail removed without a strike; YouTube looks at whether violent/gory imagery or sexualised body parts are the focal point and whether text is meant to shock. **How:** keep weapons/blood out of the focal point for PvP thumbnails. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229980?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4172. **First-strike warning and 90-day training** — a first violation usually gets a warning; a policy training lets it expire after 90 days; three strikes in 90 days can end the channel. **How:** reason to never test borderline thumbnails. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229980?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4173. **Impersonation in thumbnails** — copying another channel's branding, or using AI to copy someone's likeness so the channel looks authorised by them, is not allowed. **How:** never put a real creator's face/brand on our thumbnails; the default host is the owner's own skin. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229980?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4174. **API thumbnails.set limits** — the Data API upload accepts image/jpeg, image/png or application/octet-stream, max 50 MB, ~50 quota units per call; 429 uploadRateLimitExceeded when the channel uploaded too many recently. **How:** `POST https://www.googleapis.com/upload/youtube/v3/thumbnails/set?videoId=ID` with scope youtube.upload (needs owner OAuth; not set up here). [YouTube Data API, https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/thumbnails/set, checked 2026-10-24]
4175. **API resizes with black bars, never crops** — an uploaded thumbnail that doesn't match the dimensions is resized without changing aspect ratio, may get black bars, not cropped. **How:** always export exactly 16:9 so no bars appear. [YouTube Data API, https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/thumbnails, checked 2026-10-24]
4176. **The 5 served sizes** — video thumbnails are served as default 120x90, medium 320x180, high 480x360, standard 640x480, maxres 1280x720. **How:** preview our design at 320x180 and 120x90: `ffmpeg -i thumb.png -vf scale=320:180:flags=lanczos m.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [YouTube Data API, https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/thumbnails, checked 2026-10-24]
4177. **4:3 sizes letterbox a 16:9 design** — default/high/standard are 4:3 (120x90, 480x360, 640x480), so some surfaces/embeds get a 4:3 frame. **How:** check a 4:3 pad preview: `ffmpeg -i thumb.png -vf "scale=480:270,pad=480:360:0:45" h.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [YouTube Data API, https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/thumbnails, checked 2026-10-24]
4178. **Fetch an existing thumbnail for reference** — the API's snippet.thumbnails gives URLs per size (maxres when available). **How:** for the owner's own videos, `curl -sO https://i.ytimg.com/vi/<ID>/maxresdefault.jpg` (i.ytimg.com was allowlisted, per CLAUDE.md) to compare old vs new. [YouTube Data API, https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/thumbnails, checked 2026-10-24] [repo: CLAUDE.md "Environment facts"]

## YouTube Test & Compare (A/B)
4179. **Choose Title only, Thumbnail only, or both** — the A/B Testing button sits in the Title box or under Thumbnail; pick one of three modes and upload up to 3 variants. **How:** deliver 3 files named A/B/C; test only thumbnails if the title is fixed. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4180. **Editing during a test stops it** — changing the title or thumbnail while a test runs stops it automatically and it must be restarted. **How:** tell the owner not to tweak anything until the result. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4181. **Low-res variant downgrades all** — if any test thumbnail is under 720p (1280x720), all experiment thumbnails are downscaled to 480p (854x480). **How:** check every variant: `ffprobe -v error -show_entries stream=width,height -of csv=p=0 v*.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4182. **Not for Shorts, Premieres, scheduled lives, kids/mature/private** — Live archives can be tested; Premieres only after they become long-form; uploads up to 3 min that are square/vertical count as Shorts. **How:** don't prepare A/B variants for Shorts or the vertical promo. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4183. **Needs advanced features, desktop only** — A/B testing works only in Studio on a computer with advanced features enabled. **How:** owner sets it on desktop. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4184. **Control group sees the default** — a small share of traffic may be held back and only sees the default title/thumbnail; it's excluded from the calculation. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24] **How:** read results under Analytics > Reach > "How your A/B test is going" > Manage test.
4185. **Test old videos first** — YouTube recommends starting tests on older videos to limit the impact on overall views. **How:** first test a back-catalogue FireCraft video before a launch video. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4186. **Native vs third-party testers** — YouTube's tests are concurrent A/B/C; many third-party tools test sequentially and optimise CTR, so they can pick a different winner. **How:** trust the Studio result over sequential swaps. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4187. **Same video, different results** — results can vary like a coin flip, and early impressions come more from people who know the channel, later from new viewers. **How:** don't over-read one test; repeat the idea on another video. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4188. **Not enough impressions = no winner** — YouTube says a "Winner" is more likely the more views the video gets; low-impression videos often end Performed Same or Inconclusive. **How:** test on videos that still get impressions. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]

## YouTube's own tips and analytics
4189. **Target subscribers vs casual viewers** — for subscribers highlight familiar features (e.g. your best friend); for casual viewers show universally relatable actions and emotions (e.g. a shocked face). **How:** FireCraft returning players: familiar server landmarks; new players: a big reaction. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4190. **90% of best performers use custom thumbnails** — YouTube states 90% of the best-performing videos have custom thumbnails. **How:** always deliver a custom one with every long video. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4191. **Shoot thumbnail stills during the shoot** — take pictures during your shoot so you have several options at upload. **How:** ask the owner for 3-5 phone photos (posed reaction) per shoot. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4192. **Measure CTR on Home/Suggested in the first 24 h** — for general audiences check CTR from Home and Suggested in the first 24 h on videos with above-average impressions; for subscribers check CTR in the Subscriptions feed. **How:** ask the owner for these two numbers when judging a new style. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4193. **Use the Audience tab for ideas** — the Audience tab shows other videos your audience watched, useful for thumbnail and title ideas; research insights show what they search. **How:** owner shares a screenshot; we pull those thumbnails as style references. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]
4194. **Limit ALL CAPS and emoji in titles** — use them carefully to emphasise emotion, e.g. "Our KIDS Built A ROBOT!"; keep titles short, key words first, episode numbers and branding at the end. **How:** pair the thumbnail text with a title that doesn't repeat it. [YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en, checked 2026-10-24]

## Other platforms: covers and specs
4195. **Reel cover is cropped 3:4 on the profile grid** — Instagram's grid shows Reels at 3:4 (1080x1440) since January 2025; a 1080x1920 cover loses 240 px top and bottom. **How:** keep title and face inside the centred 1080x1440 box; preview `ffmpeg -i cover.png -vf crop=1080:1440:0:240 grid.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [Hopper HQ, https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/, checked 2026-10-24] [Oktopost, https://www.oktopost.com/blog/instagram-grid-size-guide/, checked 2026-10-24]
4196. **Reel cover cannot be changed after posting** — Instagram's Help Center (as quoted) says you can't edit the cover photo after the Reel is uploaded, so choose it before sharing. **How:** send the owner the cover file together with the video, not later. [Superprofile (quoting Instagram Help, read 14 Oct 2026), https://superprofile.bio/guides/instagram-reel-size.md, checked 2026-10-24] [Hopper HQ, https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/, checked 2026-10-24]
4197. **Reels over 3 min aren't recommended to new audiences** — per Instagram Help (as quoted), long Reels still reach followers but not non-followers. **How:** keep FireCraft promos under 3 min if reach matters. [Superprofile, https://superprofile.bio/guides/instagram-reel-size.md, checked 2026-10-24]
4198. **Reel safe zone ~950x980 centred** — Meta's percentage safe zones convert to roughly a centred 950x980 px area on 1080x1920; bottom clearance deepens on the right where buttons stack. **How:** draw a guide box when designing the cover: `ffmpeg -i cover.png -vf drawbox=x=65:y=470:w=950:h=980:color=yellow@0.6:t=4 guide.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) (box placement centred is our approximation, check it in Ads Manager's Safe zone overlay) [Hopper HQ, https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/, checked 2026-10-24]
4199. **One look across all covers** — same font, colour band and placement across Reels makes a grid look deliberate; Oktopost says to keep one primary ratio for 80% of posts. **How:** save the cover as a HyperFrames/PIL template in library/ and reuse it. [Hopper HQ, https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/, checked 2026-10-24] [Oktopost, https://www.oktopost.com/blog/instagram-grid-size-guide/, checked 2026-10-24]
4200. **Design posts at 4:5 for feed + grid** — uploading 4:5 (1080x1350) gives the most control across feed and 3:4 grid; square and landscape posts get letterboxed or cropped. **How:** carousel first slides / photo posts: 1080x1350 canvas. [Oktopost, https://www.oktopost.com/blog/instagram-grid-size-guide/, checked 2026-10-24]
4201. **TikTok: cover locked after posting** — TikTok doesn't let you edit the cover after the video is live; the workaround is re-uploading the video with a new cover. **How:** decide the cover frame before handing over the file. [Kapwing, https://www.kapwing.com/resources/how-to-change-tiktok-thumbnail/, checked 2026-10-24]
4202. **TikTok: upload a custom cover image** — on the post screen tap Edit cover, drag the timeline to a frame or tap Upload for a gallery image; add cover text in a chosen font style, pinch to resize/rotate. **How:** give the owner a 1080x1920 cover PNG in the gallery. [Kapwing, https://www.kapwing.com/resources/how-to-change-tiktok-thumbnail/, checked 2026-10-24]
4203. **Brand colour across TikTok covers** — Kapwing's example: Summer Fridays keeps a pastel green on every cover so the profile looks curated; consistency = same font, palette or recurring character. **How:** FireCraft covers: one fixed accent colour + host skin. [Kapwing, https://www.kapwing.com/resources/how-to-change-tiktok-thumbnail/, checked 2026-10-24]
4204. **Pinterest safe zones** — keep text/key elements away from: top 270 px, left 65 px, right 195 px, bottom 790 px (image and video Pins). **How:** `ffmpeg -i pin.png -vf "drawbox=x=65:y=270:w=iw-260:h=ih-1060:color=red@0.7:t=4" pinsafe.png` on 1080x1920 (tested here 2026-10-24) [Pinterest Help, https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/review-pin-specs, checked 2026-10-24]
4205. **Pinterest video Pin ratios** — 9:16 (1080x1920) for full-bleed, also 1:2, 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 1:1; MP4/M4V (MOV on mobile), H.264/H.265, 4 s to 5 min. **How:** our vertical render fits as is. [Pinterest Help, https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/review-pin-specs, checked 2026-10-24]
4206. **Pinterest tints white/black image backgrounds** — image Pins with near-white backgrounds get a 4% black layer in light mode; near-black ones get 10% white in dark mode (not videos). **How:** pure white/black cover backgrounds won't look exactly as exported; prefer a coloured background. [Pinterest Help, https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/review-pin-specs, checked 2026-10-24]
4207. **Pinterest image limits** — BMP, JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WEBP; web upload up to 20 MB; title 100 characters, description 800 (descriptions aren't shown in feeds but used for relevance). **How:** export PNG/JPG under 20 MB. [Pinterest Help, https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/review-pin-specs, checked 2026-10-24]
4208. **LinkedIn video ad thumbnail** — optional, JPG or PNG, max 2 MB, aspect ratio and resolution should match the video. **How:** same render as the video size, JPG q 2-3 under 2 MB. [LinkedIn Help, https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/85306, checked 2026-10-24]
4209. **Facebook video/Reel thumbnail** — per Ayrshare's Facebook Pages guide: no dimension requirement but same aspect ratio as the video; BMP/GIF/JPEG/PNG/TIFF, max 10 MB (Reels thumbnail JPEG/PNG, 10 MB). **How:** reuse the 16:9 or 9:16 render. [Ayrshare docs, https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/media-guidelines/facebook_pages.md, checked 2026-10-24]
4210. **Facebook feed images shown ~470 px wide** — Ayrshare: images appear in feed at max 470 px wide (page 504 px), recommended upload 1200x630, max 2048x2048. **How:** check the cover at 470 px wide: `ffmpeg -i c.png -vf scale=470:-2 fb.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [Ayrshare docs, https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/media-guidelines/facebook_pages.md, checked 2026-10-24]

## Faces, eyes and emotion
4211. **Eye contact with visible whites** — humans evolved to detect eye contact; close-ups with eye contact, especially with the whites of the eyes visible, are more eye-catching (Frederator's top tactic). **How:** pick frames where the subject looks into the lens, eyes open wide. [Tubefilter (Matt Gielen, Frederator), https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24] [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4212. **Strong emotion through empathy** — strong emotions are easily identified and the viewer feels them through empathy; target surprise, shock, empathy, excitement, confusion, intrigue. **How:** ask the owner for an exaggerated reaction photo. [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24] [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4213. **Collage of several emotions** — when one emotion isn't enough, compile several images of your face to show the range the video goes through. **How:** PIL paste 2-3 cutouts side by side. [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4214. **Familiar + unfamiliar** — a familiar face or object catches attention; pairing it with something surprising creates curiosity. **How:** host skin + one unexpected item from the video. [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4215. **Cartoon/caricature version** — turning your face or content into a cartoon is a way to switch up the look. **How:** our blocky host (`library/tools/blocky_character.py`) is exactly that. [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24] [repo: CLAUDE.md "DEFAULT HOST"]
4216. **Tight crop wins at small size** — a chest-up shot leaves the face ~30 px tall at 120 px width: reads as "a face", not an emotion; get close and let the expression read. **How:** crop so the face is large before adding text. [CreatiCalc, https://creaticalc.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-mobile-readability, checked 2026-10-24]
4217. **Two people must both fit large** — with two people in a thumbnail both faces need to stay readable; equally weighted subjects give the eye no place to land. **How:** make one dominant, the other smaller. [CreatiCalc, https://creaticalc.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-mobile-readability, checked 2026-10-24]

## Composition and focus
4218. **Sunburst behind the face** — Frederator had success with a sunburst/pinwheel background whose vanishing point sits directly behind the face, pulling the eye back to the subject. **How:** draw rays with PIL `ImageDraw.polygon` from the face centre (tested here 2026-10-24) [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24] [Pillow docs, https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/ImageDraw.html, checked 2026-10-24]
4219. **Bright backgrounds stand out on white YouTube** — bright backgrounds pop against YouTube's white page and against other thumbnails. **How:** prefer saturated background colours over dark photos for light-mode feeds. [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24]
4220. **Outline the subject** — contrast and outlines separate the subject from the background and add depth; outline layered images with a solid colour on busy backgrounds. **How:** dilate the rembg alpha and fill it white behind the cutout (see PIL section). [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24] [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4221. **Nothing important bottom-right** — YouTube covers the lower-right corner with the timestamp or watch-later button, and Western readers look there last. **How:** keep text/logo out of the bottom-right ~20%. [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24] [TubeCore, https://tubecore.xpanddigital.io/blog/youtube-thumbnail-text-guide, checked 2026-10-24]
4222. **Thumbnail should match the first 15-30 s** — viewers who feel duped click away; if the thumbnail moment is late, reorder the video or say early that it's coming. **How:** pick the thumbnail moment, then check it appears (or is announced) in the opening. [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24]
4223. **Show the result, not the process** — Frederator's rainbow-cake example: the bright shot of the finished cake with a smiling person beat a dark shot of pouring liquid. **How:** for build/PvP videos show the finished build or the win. [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24]
4224. **Copy what the target audience is trained to click** — Frederator's Toy Pizza changed to the style similar-audience channels used (full toy, muted background, text, one toy) and performance rose. **How:** pull 10 thumbnails from top Minecraft lifesteal channels as references before designing. [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24]
4225. **Small bug/logo for recognition** — a small logo or watermark signals "this is one of yours"; keep it in one fixed corner every time. **How:** FireCraft logo top-left, same size every thumbnail. [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24] [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4226. **Consistency of layout** — consistent palette, layout, number of subjects, arrangement and font make your videos identifiable; Frederator ran 8 templates over 20 months on one channel. **How:** keep one template per series. [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24]
4227. **Series/playlist themes** — give each series or playlist its own colours/layout/images for a tidy channel page. **How:** one accent colour per FireCraft series. [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4228. **Negative space** — don't fear empty space; it emphasises what matters. **How:** leave the side opposite the subject plain for text. [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4229. **Plain background for emphasis** — a solid colour or subtle pattern makes the important things obvious. **How:** rembg cutout on a flat colour canvas. [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4230. **Before/after thumbnail** — before and after photos with obvious differences make people curious about the process. **How:** `ffmpeg -i before.png -i after.png -filter_complex hstack=inputs=2,scale=1280:720 ba.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#hstack, checked 2026-10-24]
4231. **Story in several photos** — multiple images can show a progression; good for story-time videos. **How:** 3-panel `hstack=inputs=3`. [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]
4232. **Arrows lead the eye** — arrows and directional symbols guide viewers to the important part. **How:** PIL polygon arrow pointing at the key object. [Income School, https://incomeschool.com/50-great-youtube-thumbnail-design-ideas/, checked 2026-10-24]

[Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24]

## Gaze, packaging and style references
4279. **Gaze toward the object** — eye-tracking on banner ads: when a face gazed toward the product, viewers were more likely to look at it and reported higher purchase intention than with gaze away. **How:** have the host look toward the text/item, not out of frame. [Frontiers in Psychology via PMC, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5454066/, checked 2026-10-24]
4280. **Averted vs direct gaze** — the same paper cites print/banner studies where gaze at the product beat gaze at the viewer for attention to the product and brand memory. **How:** direct gaze for a reaction thumbnail; gaze-to-item when the item is the hook. [PMC5454066, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5454066/, checked 2026-10-24]
4281. **Faces beat banner blindness** — the cited literature: faces capture attention faster and more automatically than non-social objects. **How:** include a face (or the blocky host's face) when the topic allows. [PMC5454066, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5454066/, checked 2026-10-24]
4282. **Title + thumbnail before filming** — in the leaked MrBeast production guide the creative process starts with title and thumbnail; they set viewer expectations and the first minute must prove them. **How:** draft the FireCraft thumbnail idea before editing the promo. [Simon Willison on the MrBeast PDF, https://simonwillison.net/2024/Sep/15/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/, checked 2026-10-24]
4283. **Pick the more visual concept** — MrBeast: "50 Hours In My Front Yard" vs "50 Hours In Ketchup": similar effort, the ketchup image is far more clickable. **How:** choose the concept whose single image is the strangest. [Simon Willison, https://simonwillison.net/2024/Sep/15/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/, checked 2026-10-24]
4284. **CTR dictates the video** — the guide says CTR "is what dictates what we do for videos". **How:** treat the thumbnail as part of the edit, not an afterthought. [Simon Willison, https://simonwillison.net/2024/Sep/15/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/, checked 2026-10-24]
4285. **Dedicated thumbnail beats a random still** — Clipchamp's movie-poster analogy: a random still (the hero eating breakfast) won't sell an action film; make a dedicated image. **How:** never ship the auto frame. [Clipchamp, https://clipchamp.com/en/blog/make-perfect-facebook-video-thumbnail-gets-clicks/, checked 2026-10-24]
4286. **LinkedIn picks a random frame** — without a custom thumbnail LinkedIn auto-generates one from a random (maybe blurry/awkward) frame; upload JPG/PNG under 2 MB matching the video ratio. **How:** export with the video. [Brandwatch, https://www.brandwatch.com/blog/linkedin-video-specs/, checked 2026-10-24]
4287. **Gestalt figure-ground** — clear separation between subject and background is essential; desaturated backgrounds let saturated foreground elements dominate. **How:** `ffmpeg -i bg.png -vf hue=s=0.5 bgd.png` on the background layer only, then paste the cutout (tested here 2026-10-24) [Autonolab, https://blog.autonolab.com/blog/2025-09-19-thumbnail-psychology-science-of-visual-attention/, checked 2026-10-24] [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#hue, checked 2026-10-24]

## Frame grab and frame picking (ffmpeg)
4300. **Fast input seek grab** — `-ss` before `-i` seeks to the nearest seek point, and with accurate_seek (default when transcoding) decodes the gap so the frame is exact. **How:** `ffmpeg -ss 1.5 -i t.mp4 -frames:v 1 g.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html#Main-options, checked 2026-10-24]
4301. **Output-side seek** — `-ss` after `-i` decodes and discards until the time; slower on long files but simple. **How:** `ffmpeg -i t.mp4 -ss 1.5 -frames:v 1 g2.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html#Main-options, checked 2026-10-24]
4302. **Grab near the end** — `-sseof` seeks relative to the end (negative = earlier). **How:** `ffmpeg -sseof -0.5 -i t.mp4 -frames:v 1 last.png` for the payoff/freeze frame (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html#Main-options, checked 2026-10-24]
4303. **Single image with -frames:v 1** — image2 writes one file when the name has no pattern; `-frames:v 1` stops after one frame. **How:** `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -frames:v 1 img.jpeg` (tested here 2026-10-24 as g.png) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-formats.html#image2_002c-image2pipe, checked 2026-10-24]
4304. **JPEG grab quality** — `-q:v` is a codec-dependent fixed quality scale. **How:** `ffmpeg -ss 1 -i t.mp4 -frames:v 1 -q:v 2 g.jpg` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html#Main-options, checked 2026-10-24]
4305. **thumbnail filter picks a representative frame** — selects the most representative frame per batch of n frames (default 100; big n costs memory). **How:** `ffmpeg -i t.mp4 -vf thumbnail,scale=300:200 -frames:v 1 tf.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#thumbnail, checked 2026-10-24]
4306. **One candidate per batch** — `thumbnail=n=30` gives one pick per 30 frames (≈1/s at 30 fps) to review as candidates. **How:** `ffmpeg -i t.mp4 -vf thumbnail=n=30 -fps_mode passthrough tf_%02d.png` (tested here 2026-10-24: 3 files from 3 s) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#thumbnail, checked 2026-10-24]
4307. **Keyframes-only contact sheet** — `-skip_frame nokey` decodes only keyframes; tile them 8x8; `-fps_mode passthrough` stops frame duplication. **How:** `ffmpeg -skip_frame nokey -i t.mp4 -vf 'scale=128:72,tile=8x8' -an -fps_mode passthrough key%03d.png` (tested here 2026-10-24) [ffmpeg docs, https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#tile, checked 2026-10-24]

## Contrast maths
4396. **WCAG contrast ratio** — (L1+0.05)/(L2+0.05), lighter over darker, range 1:1 to 21:1. **How:** Python helper; white/black = 21.0, yellow #ffd400 on blue #1e5adc = 4.13, red on orange = 1.97 (tested here 2026-10-24) [W3C WCAG 2.1, https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/, checked 2026-10-24]
4397. **Relative luminance formula** — L = 0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B on linearised sRGB (c<=0.04045: c/12.92, else ((c+0.055)/1.055)^2.4). **How:** use it to pick text colour against the sampled background. (tested here 2026-10-24) [W3C WCAG 2.1, https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/, checked 2026-10-24]
4398. **Sample the text area** — mean colour of the region where text goes, then luminance. **How:** `np.asarray(im.crop((40,40,640,260))).reshape(-1,3).mean(0)` (tested here 2026-10-24) [W3C WCAG 2.1, https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/, checked 2026-10-24]
4399. **Complementary colour from a hex** — rotate HSV hue by 180°. **How:** `h,s,v=colorsys.rgb_to_hsv(r,g,b); colorsys.hsv_to_rgb((h+0.5)%1,s,v)` gives (220,160,30) for (30,90,220) (tested here 2026-10-24) [Python docs, https://docs.python.org/3/library/colorsys.html, checked 2026-10-24] [Tubefilter, https://www.tubefilter.com/2014/06/19/youtube-thumbnails-definitive-guide/amp/, checked 2026-10-24]
