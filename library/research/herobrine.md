# Herobrine (Minecraft legend): verified facts for explainer videos

Source: Minecraft Wiki, https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine (read 2026-10-30 with `agent-reach read`; page dated
29 Oct 2026). Used for portal order 2 "Who is Herobrine" (20 s Short). Facts below are quoted or closely paraphrased
from that page only.

- Herobrine is the subject of a community-made creepypasta and one of the major community icons of Minecraft; he has
  never been featured in the game.
- "Herobrine does not exist in any official versions of Minecraft, and never has. There are no references to him at
  all in the game's source code." Appearances come from mods or a player using a look-alike skin.
- Notch's automatic email reply: "Herobrine does not, and never has been part of Minecraft, nor do I have a dead
  brother."
- Origin: on July 31, 2010, Minecraft Forum user Flaky posted a story of a player meeting an entity with glowing white
  eyes ("White Eyes"); on August 24, 2010 a version appeared where it was a default player model with whited-out eyes.
- Brocraft streamer Copeland staged a hoax livestream on August 30 (2010) with a painting texture edited to show
  Herobrine.
- Java Edition changelogs have said "Removed Herobrine" many times as a joke, the first in Beta 1.6.6; Notch posted a
  Beta 1.7 changelog with "Removed Herobrine" at the bottom.

Not used (not verified here): any claim about Herobrine sightings being real, seeds, or "dead brother" lore as fact.
Visuals: the wiki's images are Mojang/fan content; videos use our own blocky shadow figure with white eyes instead.
